# Your First Three Bounties — Field Kit

A practical, honest workflow for landing your **first low/medium bounties** on YesWeHack.
No magic, no guarantees — just the workflow that maximizes accept rate for a beginner.

## The uncomfortable truth first
- Bounties are **confirmed by the program's triage team**, not by any tool. Your job is to
  hand them a valid, reproducible, in-scope report they can't say no to.
- Your first wins will almost always come from **recon + access-control bugs +
  misconfigurations**, not from clever memory-corruption exploits. Play the odds.
- **Everything you test must be explicitly in scope, from your own YWH account, under that
  program's rules of engagement.** Out-of-scope testing gets you banned, not paid.

## The loop
1. **Pick a program** → `01_PROGRAM_SELECTION.md` (score it before you spend an hour on it)
2. **Recon the scope** → `02_RECON_CHECKLIST.md` (map surface, find the forgotten assets)
3. **Hunt the high-ROI bug classes** → `03_BUG_CLASSES.md` (IDOR, takeover, misconfig, disclosure)
4. **Write it so triage says yes** → `04_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` (copy/paste, fill in, submit)
5. **Track it** → `05_SUBMISSION_TRACKER.md`

## Suggested first-week plan
- **Day 1–2:** Select 3 programs with the scorecard. Wildcard/large scope, rewards lows.
- **Day 3–4:** Full passive + light active recon on program #1. Log every asset.
- **Day 5–6:** Hunt IDOR and subdomain takeover across what you found. Two of the highest
  accept-rate classes for beginners.
- **Day 7:** Write and submit. Then rotate to program #2 while you wait on triage.

Realistic expectation: your first *valid* submission usually comes in the first 1–3 focused
programs if you're disciplined about recon and report quality.
