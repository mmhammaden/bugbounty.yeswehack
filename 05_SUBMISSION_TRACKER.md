## Tips while waiting on triage
- Don't spam triage for updates; give them the program's stated SLA.
- If asked for more info, respond fast and precisely — it keeps your report moving.
- A `duplicate` isn't failure; it means your methodology found a real bug. Rotate and repeat.
- Keep hunting the next program instead of refreshing the first one.
